For example, how to make a cupcake: Here’s a simple cupcake recipe, with all the steps and ingredients you need:

Ingredients
1 cup (125g) all-purpose flour
1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
1/4 cup (60g) unsalted butter, softened
1/2 cup (120ml) milk
1 large egg
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 1/2 tsp baking powder
1/4 tsp salt
Equipment
Mixing bowls
Electric mixer or whisk
Muffin tin
Cupcake liners (paper or silicone)
Spoon or ice cream scoop
Steps
Preheat Oven:
Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a muffin tin with cupcake liners.

Mix Dry Ingredients:
In a bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.

Cream Butter and Sugar:
In another bowl, beat the softened butter and sugar together until light and fluffy.

Add Egg and Vanilla:
Add the egg and vanilla extract to the butter mixture, and beat until well combined.

Combine Wet and Dry:
Add half of the dry ingredients to the wet mixture. Mix gently. Then add the milk and mix again. Finally, add the rest of the dry ingredients and mix until just combined (don’t overmix).

Fill Liners:
Spoon the batter evenly into the cupcake liners, filling each about 2/3 full.

Bake:
Bake for 15-18 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of a cupcake comes out clean.

Cool:
Let the cupcakes cool in the tin for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

Optional – Frosting:
Once cooled, you can frost your cupcakes with your favorite icing.

Enjoy your homemade cupcakes!
