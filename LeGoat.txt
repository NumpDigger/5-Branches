LeBron James is still performing at a remarkably high level in his 40s. At age 40 and in his 22nd NBA season, LeBron continues to be one of the league’s most consistent and impactful players. He’s achieved historic milestones, such as becoming the oldest player to record a 21-point triple-double in a dominant Lakers victory. His longevity and ability to play at an All-NBA level are widely praised, and he credits his success to a disciplined routine, training, and evolving approach to the game. LeBron is not only maintaining his performance but, in some aspects, even outperforming his prime years, according to recent stats and analyses.
